Private by design. Cards are encrypted between players.

Neutral by construction. The relay is transport, never dealer.

Final by consensus. Stakes settle against verified outcomes.

The hand stays between players.
The settlement doesn’t.

Poker needs hidden information. Blockchains need public agreement. BitPoker separates the two, keeping gameplay peer-to-peer while using the chain for matchmaking, escrow, settlement, and the exceptional dispute path.

  1. 01

    Match & lock

    Compatible game intents create a session and lock both stakes in escrow.

  2. 02

    Shuffle & play

    Mental-poker cryptography deals the hand over an assigned, untrusted relay.

  3. 03

    Sign & settle

    Matching signed results release escrow with exact chip conservation.

  4. 04

    Prove when needed

    Evidence is replayed by the deterministic C++ engine inside validator consensus.

“Trust the protocol” should be a claim you can test.
